Information sheet

You are invited to take part in a research study. Please read the description carefully. If you are happy to participate, click the 'Continue' button at the bottom of the page.

What is the purpose of this study?

This study will help us understand how people make decisions and learn when interacting with unfamiliar environments. Improving our understanding of these processes could have implications for education, health, and everyday decision-making.

What is involved in the study?

To start, we will explain a decision-making task and ask a few comprehension questions so that we can be sure you understand the task before you begin. You will then complete two parts of the task, each followed by a short set of questions about what you learned. The whole study should take approximately 25 minutes.

Is there a performance related bonus associated with this study?

Yes. Please see the Prolific advertisement for details. The more points you earn overall, the higher the bonus. We aim to credit any performance related bonus via Prolific within one working week.

What if I don't want to take part?

Your participation is completely voluntary, so it is up to you whether you would like to take part. You can withdraw from the study at any point without having to provide any explanation. After the study is complete, you can withdraw your data for up to four weeks by contacting the research team (contact details below). After four weeks, data will be fully anonymised and it will no longer be possible to identify your individual data.

Will my taking part in the study be kept confidential?

Yes, your responses and data will be treated as strictly confidential. We request only your Prolific ID and signature as part of the consent process. If you are conducting this study via Prolific, or via a similar platform which holds a record of participant IP addresses, note that your IP address will not be transferred and stored outside of Prolific, for instance it will not be stored in the dataset used for analysis.

What will happen to my data?

The Data Protection Act 2018 and UK General Data Protection Regulations allow you control over your personal data and how it is used. Under GDPR, Bangor University is the data controller in relation to your personal data and will process your data for the purposes of this study. Digital research data will be stored on a password-protected computer. At the end of the study, the anonymised dataset may be deposited on a publicly available data repository so that other scientists can examine our results.

Who is running the study?

This study is led by Dr Sam Jones of Bangor University's Department of Psychology. The study has been given a favourable opinion by a Bangor University Research Ethics Committee.

Who do I contact if I have concerns?

Instructions

You will see a machine with six symbols on it. Underneath each symbol is a window that lights up to show what that symbol is worth.

Below the symbols are four action switches, labelled A1 to A4. The machine accepts exactly two switches at a time, and each pair of switches is wired to one particular symbol. That symbol is worth a fixed number of points — some are worth positive points, some negative.

Pressing TEST lights up the symbol that pair is wired to and shows you what it is worth. Each press of TEST costs you 2 points.

Pressing EXECUTE commits to the pair you have set: you collect that symbol's points and the round ends.

Your score for a round is the points you collect minus what you spent on testing. You do not have to test before you execute.

Your goal is to earn as many points as you can. The more points you earn, the higher your performance related bonus.

We will ask you a few questions about the machine afterwards, but your bonus for this part comes from the points you collect.

A note on how to take part. This task is designed to be done from memory alone. Please don't jot anything down or use any other aid to keep track of the machine — we're interested in what people naturally remember, and responses suggesting otherwise may unfortunately have to be excluded. Thank you for helping us keep the data clean.

Part two

That is the end of part one. Part two works differently.

You will see a new machine. It works in the basically same way, but it has different symbols, the switches are wired up differently, and the windows are worth different amounts. This means that nothing you learned about the first machine will help you here.

Importantly, there are no costs in part two. Pressing TEST is free, and you are not collecting points. The EXECUTE button is locked.

Your job in this phase is to explore the machine and learn how it works prior to a test.

Take your time and test every combination of switches until you understand it. When you have explored enough (you must TEST each combination of switches at least twice), a button will appear to let you continue.

Afterwards you will answer the same kinds of questions as in part one. Crucially though, in part two that is how you earn your points: you score a point for every test question you get right.

A reminder. As before, please explore and answer from memory alone, without notes or any other aid. We're interested in what you naturally remember, and responses suggesting otherwise may unfortunately have to be excluded. Thank you.
